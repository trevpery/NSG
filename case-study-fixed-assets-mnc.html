Fixed Asset Verification — Financial Technology MNC

Background

Our client, a globally renowned player in ATMs, POS, Security systems was desirous of having its Fixed Assets at its work sites across India physically verified. Our firm was assigned the responsibility of carrying out this exercise.

Coverage

Over 30 locations spread across India

Scope of our audit

Physical verification of the client's Fixed Assets & reconciliation with the FA register.

Key achievements

Foremost, we planned the manner in which we would cover all the designated locations.

We identified our Associates who were ideally placed near these locations & obtained confirmations re: their participation.

The assignment got executed within the agreed timelines.

Our team's findings helped in significantly improving the FA register - assets listed in wrong locations, assets listed but not found, assets found but not listed, assets found to be damaged.

The client expressed its satisfaction with our efforts / reports & appreciated the approach taken by us to execute the assignment in a timely, smooth manner.
